About Form8846Calculator

Why This Site Exists

Form 8846 is a one-page form, yet most free explanations either bury the math inside long payroll-software pages or require an email address before showing a result. This site exists to give restaurant and bar owners a clean, no-login calculator and plain-English reference pages for the FICA tip credit. The calculator runs in the browser; nothing is stored.

How We Verify Rates

Each year we check the following sources and update the pages that depend on them:

  • IRS Form 8846 and its instructions
  • IRC §45B and related regulations
  • Social Security Administration wage-base announcements
  • U.S. Department of Labor tipped-employee minimum-wage table
  • State labor-department notices for cash-wage changes

Every content page carries a "Last updated" date. Figures that can change mid-year are confirmed against the current official notice before publication.
